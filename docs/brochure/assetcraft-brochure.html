Fixed assets with Austrian AfA, software licences and expiry dates for a small business. One SQLite file, one binary that is both CLI and MCP server. Part of the back-office craft suite (replaces the asset Excel, the licence spreadsheet and the calendar reminders for certificates and Pickerl).

Status: v0.1.1, Tier 1 (store + CLI + MCP, tested). No desktop window yet. Synthetic demo data only; AfA rules not reviewed by a Steuerberater.

  • How to use it day to day: HELP.md
  • What it is for and where it stops: docs/PRD.md
  • Every MCP tool: docs/TOOLS.md (generated, checked by a test)
  • Changes: CHANGELOG.md

What it does

  • Assets: tag, category, in-service date, cost (the AfA base), holder, location, serial, warranty, supplier, invoice reference, maintenance log.
  • AfA (EStG section 7), in cents: straight-line, calendar fiscal year, Halbjahres-AfA in the first and in the disposal year, GWG write-off at or below the limit (default 1.000,00 since 2023, configurable). The remainder cent goes to the final year, so a schedule always sums to the cost and never grows a stray year.
  • Categories: default life and minimum life (cars: at least 8 years, EStG section 8(6)) and the EKR asset account (IT 0620, car 0630, BGA 0660, GWG 0680). Editable.
  • buchcraft link: afa 2026 --emit sends one asset.depreciation_due per asset to buchcraft (7000 or 7010 against the asset account). buchcraft turns them into drafts a human approves. Each asset/year is sent once; after that the AfA basis is frozen (in code and by a SQLite trigger).
  • Disposal: disposal-year AfA, book value, gain or loss; the asset becomes read-only.
  • Licences: seats, assignments (refused when full unless forced), renewal dates, cost per used seat. Keys are never stored, only where they live; a pasted key is refused.
  • Expiries: warranties, licence renewals and deadlines (certificates, domains, insurance, inspections) due within N days, overdue first.
  • Labels: printable 60 x 25 mm SVG with a QR code (ASSET:<tag>).
  • CSV import: tag;name;category;in_service;cost[;serial;location;holder], all-or-nothing, with --dry-run. Gets a real inventory in on day one.

Quick start

assetcraft demo-seed
assetcraft list
assetcraft schedule NAS-001
assetcraft afa 2026                      # preview
assetcraft afa 2026 --emit               # drafts for buchcraft
buchcraft inbox-drain; buchcraft drafts list; buchcraft drafts approve 3
assetcraft expiries --days 60
assetcraft license audit
assetcraft add GOL-002 "Mini PC" --category it --in-service 09.11.2026 --cost "689,00"
assetcraft label GOL-001 D:\Temp\gol-001.svg
assetcraft import D:\Temp\inventory.csv --dry-run

Product Requirements & Architecture Specification

What it is

Fixed assets with Austrian AfA, software licences and expiry dates for a small business. Pure Rust, ArtCraft pattern. Suite plan: BACKOFFICE_CRAFT_SUITE.md, section 4.4.

Users

  • The owner who has to know what the business owns, what it is worth and what expires.
  • Agents over MCP: register purchases, run the year-end AfA, watch expiries and licence seats.
  • buchcraft (via the bus) and, through it, the Steuerberater.

Jobs

1. Register assets once and get a correct AfA plan.

2. Post year-end AfA to the books without retyping.

3. Not overpay or under-license software.

4. Never miss a warranty, certificate, domain, insurance or inspection date.

Today (v0.1.0)

  • Assets with categories (default and minimum life, EKR account), maintenance log, disposal with gain/loss.
  • AfA engine in cents: Halbjahres-AfA, GWG with configurable limit, remainder to the final year.
  • afa --emit -> buchcraft drafts -> approved -> booked (verified end to end); posted AfA basis frozen by trigger.
  • Licences with seats, assignment guard, audit; keys never stored.
  • Expiries across warranties, renewals and deadlines; QR SVG labels; all-or-nothing CSV import.
  • 24 MCP tools (TOOLS.md, generated and test-checked).

Boundaries

  • Calendar fiscal year, straight-line AfA only.
  • No direct booking: buchcraft receives drafts that a human approves.
  • Not tax advice; categories and lives are defaults to check with the Steuerberater.

Gaps (honest)

  • No egui window.
  • Degressive AfA, Luxustangente (car cost cap), IFB and non-calendar fiscal years not modelled.
  • Disposal sends no event (residual value booked by hand).
  • No direct import from rust-devices-mcp or winops inventory (CSV only).
  • SVG labels only, no label-printer driver; synthetic data only.

Next

1. egui app: register table, asset detail with schedule chart, expiry board, label print.

2. Steuerberater review; degressive AfA as an option.

3. Real inventory import from the fleet (Goliath, robots, plugs).
